How to help

You are invited to test our software and report bugs. Questions may be asked in our mailing list.

We currently follow a development model of "Benevolent Dictatorship". This is for historical reasons. Most contributions come from the project founder who implements ideas which he formulated in his doctoral thesis.

Things like example applications or documentation are added from time to time by various university students. However, the programming approach of CYBOP is so different, that a steady community could not be formed yet.

Even though large parts of our software are already functioning and usable e.g. for scripting or with text user interface, the project is still in research state and possibly not attractive to most.
