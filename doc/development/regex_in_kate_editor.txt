
Regular Expressions (RegEx) in Kate Text Editor
===============================================

Include
_______

Find sequence "level_log_cyboi_model.c" containing line break:
.*level_log_cyboi_model.c"\n+

Find sequence containing "#include" and "constant/" and line break:
#include+.+constant/+.*\n+

Parametres of "modify"
______________________

Example:
- an additional parametre is required
- therefore, one more null pointer value has to be handed over
- one *NULL_POINTER_STATE_CYBOI_MODEL gets replaced by two

Krusader Menu:
> Extras > Suchen (Strg + S)

Search:
modify_item(

Open:
all search result files in Kate text editor

Kate Menu:
> Bearbeiten > In Dateien suchen (Strg + Umschalt + F)

Replace:
, *NULL_POINTER_STATE_CYBOI_MODEL, (void*) APPEND_MODIFY_LOGIC_CYBOI_FORMAT);

with:
, *NULL_POINTER_STATE_CYBOI_MODEL, *NULL_POINTER_STATE_CYBOI_MODEL, (void*) APPEND_MODIFY_LOGIC_CYBOI_FORMAT);

Replace:
, *NULL_POINTER_STATE_CYBOI_MODEL, (void*) OVERWRITE_MODIFY_LOGIC_CYBOI_FORMAT);

with:
, *NULL_POINTER_STATE_CYBOI_MODEL, *NULL_POINTER_STATE_CYBOI_MODEL, (void*) OVERWRITE_MODIFY_LOGIC_CYBOI_FORMAT);
